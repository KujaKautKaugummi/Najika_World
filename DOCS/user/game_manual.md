# Najika World - Game Manual

Welcome to Najika World! This manual will help you get started with both the Mobile Game and Web Game.

---

## 🎮 Getting Started

### Mobile Game (UE5 - Android)

1. **Install the APK** on your Android device (Xiaomi 11T Pro recommended)
2. **Grant permissions** for camera, microphone, storage
3. **Create your character** - Choose species, name, and appearance
4. **Start your adventure!**

### Web Game (Digivice - Browser)

1. **Open browser** and navigate to the game URL
2. **Login/Register** with your account
3. **Choose mode:**
   - **Digivice Mode** - Room-based gameplay
   - **Open World Mode** - Explore the vast world with 9 regions

---

## 🌍 Game Modes

### Digivice Mode (Room-Based)
- Home screen with your character
- Feed, train, and interact with your character
- Access mini-games and features
- Manage inventory and equipment

### Open World Mode (3D Exploration)
- Explore 9 unique regions with different biomes
- Dynamic weather and day/night cycle
- Encounter wild creatures and battle
- Build cities and customize your world

---

## ⚔️ Combat System

### Battle Basics
- **HP (Health Points)** - When it reaches 0, you faint
- **Attack** - Determines damage dealt
- **Defense** - Reduces incoming damage
- **Speed** - Determines turn order

### Actions
1. **Attack** - Basic attack
2. **Skill** - Use special abilities (costs MP/SP)
3. **Item** - Use items from inventory
4. **Flee** - Attempt to escape (success based on Speed)

### Nemesis Arena (Shadow of Mordor-style)
- **Promote enemies** to Nemesis status
- **Nemesis remember** your past battles
- **Grudge system** - Nemeses seek revenge
- **Brutal finishers** - 10 categories of finishing moves

---

## 🎒 Inventory & Items

### Item Types
- **Consumables** - Health potions, mana potions, food
- **Equipment** - Weapons, armor, accessories
- **Materials** - Crafting ingredients
- **Special** - Quest items, keys

### Equipment Slots
- **Weapon** - Primary weapon (sword, staff, etc.)
- **Armor** - Body armor (chest, legs, etc.)
- **Accessory 1** - Ring, necklace, etc.
- **Accessory 2** - Additional accessory

---

## 🏝️ World Regions & Biomes

### 9 Main Regions
1. **Forest** - Green plains, abundant vegetation
2. **Desert** - Hot, sandy dunes
3. **Ice** - Frozen tundra, snow-covered
4. **Volcano** - Lava flows, ash clouds
5. **Coast** - Beaches, ocean views
6. **Mountain** - Rocky peaks, high altitude
7. **Swamp** - Murky waters, dense fog
8. **Highland** - Rolling hills, grasslands
9. **Caves** - Underground tunnels, darkness

### Dynamic Features
- **Weather** - Rain, snow, fog, clear
- **Day/Night Cycle** - 24-hour cycle with changing light
- **Biome Transitions** - Smooth blending between regions
- **Ambient Sounds** - Unique audio for each biome

---

## 🎤 Voice Chat

### Using Voice Features
1. **Click microphone icon** in the UI
2. **Speak** into your device microphone
3. **Voice is transcribed** using Whisper AI
4. **AI responds** with text-to-speech

### Voice Commands
- "Show inventory"
- "Go to [location]"
- "Attack [enemy]"
- "Use [item]"

## 🛠️ Controls

### Keyboard (Web Game)
- **WASD** - Move character
- **Space** - Jump
- **Shift** - Sprint
- **E** - Interact
- **I** - Open inventory
- **M** - Open map
- **Tab** - Toggle menu

### Mouse (Web Game)
- **Left Click** - Select/Attack
- **Right Click** - Camera rotate
- **Scroll Wheel** - Zoom in/out

### Touch (Mobile)
- **Virtual Joystick** - Movement
- **Buttons** - Jump, attack, interact
- **Pinch** - Zoom
- **Swipe** - Camera control

## 📊 Progression

### Leveling Up
- Gain **Experience Points (XP)** from battles and quests
- Level up to increase stats:
  - HP increases
  - Attack increases
  - Defense increases
  - Speed increases
- Learn new skills at certain levels

### Character Growth
- **Training** - Improve stats through mini-games
- **Evolution** - Some species can evolve at certain levels
- **Equipment** - Upgrade gear for better stats
- **Skills** - Unlock new abilities

## 🏆 Achievements

Track your progress with achievements:
- **Explorer** - Visit all 9 regions
- **Master Trainer** - Reach max level
- **Arena Champion** - Defeat 100 nemeses
- **Collector** - Obtain all items
- **Voice Master** - Use voice commands 1000 times

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do I save my progress?**
A: Progress auto-saves every 5 minutes and when you quit.

**Q: Can I play offline?**
A: Web game requires internet. Mobile game has limited offline mode.

**Q: How do I change my character?**
A: Access Settings → Character → Change Species

**Q: Where can I find rare items?**
A: Check dungeons, defeat bosses, and complete quests.

**Q: How does the Nemesis Arena work?**
A: Defeat an enemy, then "Promote to Nemesis" to add them to your arena.
